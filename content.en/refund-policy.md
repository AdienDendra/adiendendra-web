**Effective date: 9 September 2026**

This Refund Policy applies to purchases of AD Skin Tools. Contact: [hello@adiendendra.com](mailto:hello@adiendendra.com).

## Evaluate before purchasing

AD Skin Tools provides a full-featured 48-hour trial so customers can test its workflow and compatibility before purchasing. Customers should use the trial with a non-confidential Maya scene and verify the supported Maya version, operating system, and processor architecture.

## Digital purchases and change of mind

Because AD Skin Tools is a digitally delivered software product and the full-featured trial is available before purchase, completed purchases are generally final after the product has been delivered or the licence has been activated.

A refund is not generally provided solely because a customer:

- Changes their mind.
- No longer needs or wants the product.
- Did not use the available trial.
- Selected an unsupported or incorrect environment despite the published compatibility information.
- Finds another product or price they prefer.

This change-of-mind position does not override rights that apply by law.

## Faults, incorrect description, and mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or modifies consumer guarantees or other rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Depending on the circumstances, applicable consumer law may provide a repair, replacement, refund, cancellation, or another remedy where a product has a qualifying fault, is materially different from its description, or cannot perform its stated purpose.

In Australia, these rights may include remedies available under the Australian Consumer Law. The appropriate remedy depends on the nature and seriousness of the problem.

## Requesting help or a refund review

First contact [hello@adiendendra.com](mailto:hello@adiendendra.com) with:

- The Lemon Squeezy order reference.
- Maya version, operating system, and processor architecture.
- A clear description of the problem and any relevant error message.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

Do not include the complete licence key or confidential production assets in the initial email.

Where appropriate, an installation, licensing, or compatibility issue may be investigated before a refund decision is made. This does not delay or remove any remedy that must be provided under applicable law.

## Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record

Lemon Squeezy is the Merchant of Record and processes approved refunds, payment reversals, and chargebacks. Refund availability and processing are also subject to applicable law and Lemon Squeezy's policies and decisions. Bank or payment-provider processing times may apply after a refund is issued.

See Lemon Squeezy's information about [refunds and chargebacks](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/payments/refunds-chargebacks).

## Effect on the licence

If a refund, chargeback, payment reversal, or order invalidation is actually processed, the corresponding AD Skin Tools licence may be suspended or revoked. Protected product operations may then stop working.
