On this 18th of October, 2026, I want to thank you for keeping alive the flame I once placed within your heart. I have so often failed to be there for you, yet you continued to care for its gentle glow, never letting it fade, remaining faithful without resentment.

Let these words come softly, like a traveler gently knocking at the door of your heart. With weary steps, carrying gratitude for the long, endless seasons we have walked through together.

My dearest love, forgive me for the times I was too frail to feel your sorrow, for failing to soothe the wounds you carried, and touched the blisters that were just beginning to heal. I thought I knew the path to your healing, when all along, I was lost in the quiet corridors of your sorrow.

But do you ever wonder? 

When the world lets go of its endless voices, there are verses of love still whispering in the silence of my heart, and when all that is fleeting seems to fall asleep, there is the quietest prayer, a tender whisper of love that words could never quite express.
Believe me.. in every prayer I whisper in the last third of the night, it is your name that lingers the longest..

Adien Dendra
