This page provides the essential information needed to install, evaluate, and activate AD Skin Tools. Detailed feature instructions will be added as the product approaches release.

For features, pricing, compatibility, and availability, see the [AD Skin Tools product page](/en/3dtools/ad-skin-tools/).

## Installation

AD Skin Tools will be delivered as a downloadable ZIP for a specific Autodesk Maya version, operating system, and processor architecture. Select the package that matches your environment and follow the installation instructions included with the download.

Autodesk Maya is required separately and is not included with the product.

## Starting the 48-hour trial

The full-featured trial does not begin automatically when Maya opens. It starts only after you select **Start 48-Hour Trial** inside AD Skin Tools.

- An internet connection is required to start the trial.
- The trial lasts 48 hours from the recorded start time.
- One trial is available per device.
- Reinstalling the package does not restart the trial.

After the trial expires, protected operations require a paid licence.

## Activating a paid licence

After purchasing, enter the licence key supplied through the checkout and delivery process into the AD Skin Tools licence window. An internet connection is required for activation.

An Individual License can be active on up to two devices belonging to the customer. It is not a shared two-user or team licence.

## Changing devices

Use the deactivation option in the licence window before moving an activation to another computer. Deactivating a device releases its slot so the licence can be activated elsewhere.

If the original device is no longer accessible, contact [hello@adiendendra.com](mailto:hello@adiendendra.com) with the order reference and a brief explanation. Do not send the full licence key by email.

## Working offline

The tool normally refreshes its online validation every seven days. After a successful validation, a paid licence can continue operating offline for up to 30 days. Once that offline period ends, AD Skin Tools must connect successfully before protected operations can continue.

## Support and policies

For installation, activation, compatibility, or product-related technical issues, contact [hello@adiendendra.com](mailto:hello@adiendendra.com).
