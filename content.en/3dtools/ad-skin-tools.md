## Features

- **Focused skin setup:** Bind Maya geometry and add influences to an existing skinCluster without switching between separate workflows.
- **Direct weight editing:** Flood and smooth weights while staying focused on the selected geometry or components.
- **Clear weight inspection:** Visualise skin-weight distribution directly in the viewport while reviewing deformation.
- **Preview before mirroring:** Register influence pairings, inspect the mirror preview, and apply skin weights only after reviewing the result.
- **Preview before transfer:** Review skin weights from donor surfaces before applying them to target meshes.
