## Key Workflows

- **Regional Flood:** Recalculate regions owned by selected bind influences. Component selection and Maya Soft Selection define the affected scope, with Blend and Iterations controlling the result.
- **Smooth:** Relax weights on selected components or across the loaded mesh while respecting locked influences and Soft Selection falloff.
- **Bind and Add Influence:** Bind geometry and add influences to an existing skinCluster from the same tool interface.
- **Skin Weight Visual:** Inspect weight distribution directly in the Maya viewport. The visual display is temporary and does not change stored skin weights.
- **Mirror on posed meshes:** Preview and register vertex pairing before mirroring weights. Registered pairing can be reused on a posed mesh without returning the character to its bind pose.
- **Transfer with Preview:** Register skinned source surfaces and target meshes, inspect the transfer correspondence, then apply weights to eligible components or whole meshes.

## Installation and Activation

1. Download the ZIP that matches the Maya installation and follow its included installation instructions.
2. Open AD Skin Tool in Maya, then open the **License** window.
3. Select **Start 48-Hour Trial** to begin the trial, or use **Buy License** and **Activate License** for a purchased licence.

Freemius sends the licence key to the email address used at checkout. The [License Guide](/en/documentation/ad-skin-tool-license/) covers activation, device deactivation, validation, offline access, and troubleshooting.

## Online Validation and Offline Access

A paid licence normally validates online every **seven days**. Each successful online check refreshes offline access for up to **30 days**. Once that period ends without a successful connection, online validation is required before licensed features can continue.

The perpetual licence itself does not expire when the offline-access period ends. Further details are available in the [License Guide](/en/documentation/ad-skin-tool-license/).

## Common Questions

**Does the trial start automatically?**

No. The 48-hour trial begins only after **Start 48-Hour Trial** is selected in the **License** window.

**Is a permanent internet connection required?**

No. A valid paid licence supports offline use within the current 30-day access period. Reconnecting allows the next online validation to refresh that period.

**Can the licence be moved to another computer?**

Yes. Deactivate an unused computer to free one of the two device slots, then activate the new computer using the same licence key. The [License Guide](/en/documentation/ad-skin-tool-license/) explains the process.
